## Technical Notes
- Monthly Chanda uses `purposeCode=MONTHLY_CHANDA`, `frequency=MONTHLY`, `settlementPolicy=OLDEST_FIRST`
- Dues are generated for ALL_ELIGIBLE members (all ACTIVE members in the masjid)
- FIFO allocation automatically distributes payments to oldest periods first
- Dues status: PENDING → PARTIAL → PAID (not "SETTLED")
- Periods are stored with IST-midnight timestamps but compared with UTC dates from UI
